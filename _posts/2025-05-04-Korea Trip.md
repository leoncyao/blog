Lessons learned:

- Busan and Incheon don't take credit cards

Met up with:

I saw a baseball game in Korea. All the teams are owned by corporations, and every single player has their own chant that the diehard fans memorize.

It was a bit disconcerting to hear what some of the lyrics meant:
- "We can't live without you LG!!"

Imagine rows of children chanting this.
